GOT QUESTIONS?

Everything you need to know before starting a project.

What type of videos do you edit?

Harshit edits YouTube videos, Instagram Reels, Shorts, cinematic videos, promotional content and more.

Which editing software do you use?

Harshit primarily uses CapCut along with creative editing, color grading, visual effects and sound design techniques.

How do I send my footage?

After discussing the project, footage can be shared through a suitable cloud-storage or file-transfer service.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Revisions depend on the editing package selected. The number of included revisions is shown in the pricing section.

How much does video editing cost?

Pricing depends on the type, length and complexity of the project. Check the packages above or contact Harshit for a custom quote.
